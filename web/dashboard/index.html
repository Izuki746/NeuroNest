    How NeuroNest works

    After a stroke, the brain can rewire itself, but only if it gets the right help at the right time. NeuroNest is a simulator to find that help.

    1. 1 · The strokeA virtual human brain (built from real MRI wiring data) has a stroke in the left motor area. The dark dots are damaged, so the right arm is weak.
    2. 2 · RehabSqueeze your hand at the camera. Each squeeze is the patient trying to move. The signal travels through the brain, and the stroke blocks most of it.
    3. 3 · Stimulation, at the right timeLight stimulation (blue = excite, yellow = calm) just before a squeeze helps the brain build new routes around the damage. White lines = new connections.
    4. 4 · The AIIt predicts how well this patient will recover in 3 months, finds the best treatment by testing hundreds on copies of their brain, and spots seizures before they happen.

    A research simulator, not a medical device. No real patients or animals involved.
